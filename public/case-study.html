SpendWise

An AI-powered personal finance app that predicts your month, flags the small “leak” expenses draining your account, and gently nudges you to invest the difference — so growing money feels calm, not stressful.

👤 Young professionals · 22–30 · India 📱 Mobile-first (iPhone 15/16 · 393×852) 🌱 New to investing ₹ Rupee-native

Overview

The one-line idea

Most money apps show you the past — what you already spent. SpendWise looks forward: it predicts the month ahead, points out the tiny recurring spends you’ve forgotten about, and turns that wasted money into a small, low-risk investment. One calm loop: predict → spot leaks → divert to invest.

01

Predict

An AI forecast of the full month’s spend, by category, in plain language.

02

Spot leaks

Flags unused subscriptions and creeping habits — money slipping away quietly.

03

Divert

One tap moves those leaks into a low-risk fund. Growth, without the anxiety.

The problem

Money is stressful when you’re just starting out

India’s young earners are financially capable but anxious. They save when they can, but investing feels like a locked door — full of jargon, risk, and fear of “doing it wrong.”

What they feel

  • “I earn okay, but I don’t know where it goes.”
  • “Investing sounds smart, but also scary and complicated.”
  • “Budgeting apps make me feel guilty, not in control.”

Where apps fall short

  • They report the past instead of preparing you for what’s next.
  • They shout in red and use finance jargon.
  • They stop at “here’s your spending” — no bridge to actually growing money.

Design goals

Reduce money anxiety, on every screen

Every decision laddered back to one north star: make a nervous first-time investor feel calm and in control.

One clear action per screen

Never more than one primary button. The next right step is always obvious.

Plain, friendly language

“Leaks,” “divert to invest,” “you’re on track” — no CAGR, no NAV, no jargon.

Large, legible numbers

The number you care about is the biggest thing on the screen, in tabular figures.

Calm, not corporate

Soft neutrals, generous whitespace, one confident brand colour — reassuring, not loud.

Components & foundations

Reusable set: buttons (primary / secondary), inputs, cards, list rows with amounts, chips (category & risk), bottom tab bar, donut / bar / area charts, bottom sheets.

Shape & space: rounded corners, soft subtle shadows, an 8-pt spacing system, and generous padding for breathing room.

Frame: a true iPhone 15/16 canvas — 393 × 852 pt — so the design maps 1:1 to a real device.

Flows & key decisions

25 screens, six flows

Onboarding plus five tabs — Home, Budget, Invest, Reports, Profile. A few decisions that shaped the work:

1.1 · Onboarding

A calm, two-minute setup

One question per screen, a progress bar, and a clear reason for every permission we ask for.

Decision: the account-linking screen leads with “Read-only — SpendWise can never move your money,” because trust is the biggest barrier for a first-time user handing over bank access.
2.1.1 · Home

Spend prediction — the heart of the app

The predicted month up top, a category donut, then leak flags, then the divert-to-invest nudge — in that order.

Decision: leaks are framed as gentle nudges, never accusations — “these are just nudges, nothing happens automatically.” Anxiety down, agency up.
2.1.3 · Invest

Low-risk first, jargon never

Recommendations are ranked by comfort, a simulator lets you see growth before committing, and round-ups automate the habit.

Decision: the simulate-before-you-commit step exists specifically to defuse fear — you watch the money grow on a slider before any real decision.
2.1.2 · Budget

Alerts that inform, not alarm

An unusual-spend alert reads “that’s a bigger spend than usual” — a heads-up, not a telling-off.

Decision: colour is never used alone. Every warning pairs its amber/red with text and an icon, so meaning survives for colour-blind users.
2.1.4–5 · Reports & Profile

The long view, and control

A six-month spend-vs-save history and an AI forecast of next month — explained in words, not just charts. Profile keeps goals, linked accounts, and calm-by-default notifications in one place.

Accessibility

Designed to be usable by more people

Contrast

Strong text contrast against soft neutral surfaces for comfortable reading.

Never colour alone

Status is always carried by text + icon as well as colour.

Tap targets ≥ 44px

Comfortable, thumb-friendly hit areas throughout the mobile UI.

What I’d explore next

If this went further

  • Usability testing with 5–6 first-time investors to pressure-test the “divert” language and the trust framing on account linking.
  • A dark theme and dynamic type sizes for accessibility.
  • Regional language support (Hindi, and one South-Indian language) given the target audience.
  • A goal-progress celebration moment to reinforce the savings habit over time.
